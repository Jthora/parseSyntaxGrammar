Quantum Grammar Facts, Good Bad & Ugly

Quantum Grammar facts good, bad & ugly are like marathon-runner-wanna-bees with only 20 minutes a week to spare, who dream of making changes….

There’s people wanna run a marathon.

So they go along to the local gym, and book a personal trainer.

“I’ve only got 20 minutes a week to spare,” they tell the trainer.

“Well,” says the personal trainer, “that’s not gunna work… Not if you’re serious about wanting to run a marathon. You’ve got to train every day. Otherwise you’re wasting your time…”

Stop kidding yourself.

You’re not that serious.

It’s a decision.

Not a debate.

Here’s the bad news:

Same with learning Quantum Grammar.

After 90,000 hours and over 2500 seminars, DWM claims it takes most people around 200 hours of study before they get a handle on his technology.

Let’s do the math.

Say you’re really, really busy, and you can only find a spare 20 minutes/ week to learn Quantum Grammar…

So, to complete your 200 hours of training… It’s gunna take you 11 years…

Problem is, every week, you;ll be like Drew Barrimore in 50 First Dates… Every week you’re going to be (almost) starting over.

Because you’ll have forgotten what you studied last week.

It’s not going to work.

Forget it.

Just get on with your life and stop kidding yourself.

It’s not going to happen.

But here’s the Good News about learning quantum grammar…

If you really want to learn Correct-Sentence-Structure-Communication-Parse-Syntax-Grammar, then try this plan:

Get up an hour earlier.

And study Quantum Grammar for that hour.

Do this five days a week.

Or get up 30 minutes earlier.

Until you can make it a habit.

Then push yourself to get up another 30 minutes earlier.

Quantum Grammar Facts Good, Bad & Ugly 

Everyone has 24 hours in a day, 168 hours in a week.

And everyone who’s really committed, WILL find 5 hours a week to learn quantum grammar.

Why 5 hours?

In 8 months, you’ll have achieved 200 hours.

But seriously, you’re probably wasting your time.

You’re better off finding 10 hours a week, for 20 weeks.

Or just forget about it.

Because it’s not going to happen.

And you’re just kidding yourself about learning Correct-Sentence-Structure-Communication-Parse-Syntax-Grammar.

It’ll be more like a hobby. Something to tinker with when you’re bored.

Good Quantum Grammar study takes time. Just like running that marathon.

You need to do it every day.

And if you think you don’t have ANY free time, then keep a time-sheet for a week. Write down everything you do for seven days, and see where you can find 5 hours.

Ok, so you’re a full-time parent, with a house full of hungry mouths to feed, and more clothes to wash than a hotel laundromat…

And you just don’t have any spare time.

Well, there’s another option.

If you’re serious.

It’s a decision.

Not a debate.

If you think you can’t find the time or you’re “too busy”… Stop wasting your time and kidding yourself that you really want to make these changes in your life.

Forget about learning quantum grammar or Correct-Sentence-Structure-Communication-Parse-Syntax-Grammar.

And get on with what’s really important in your life, without stressing out over learning quantum grammar.

Give yourself permission to move on, and not worry that this technology needs to be shared far and wide.

And time’s running out.
